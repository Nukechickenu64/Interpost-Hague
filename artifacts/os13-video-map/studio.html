Video → Tilemap Studio

Reconstruct SS13/OS13 maps from local recordings. Detect camera movement, reconnect repeated locations, and review unresolved scenes before treating them as actual Z levels. All processing stays on your computer.

Download YouTube footage to a local file first: yt-dlp -S vcodec:h264,res:1080 --merge-output-format mp4 -o map.mp4 "https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kRaesf5j1h0"

1. Load videos & pick the game viewport

Repair an existing all_z_layers.zip export

Import a ZIP from an earlier run. All visible pixels and region boundaries are restored, and you can reconnect overlapping regions by clicking matching landmarks or running the overlap finder. It will not recover scenes that were never captured.

Add local videos, then seek to a frame with the game visible. Drag on the preview below to select the game viewport; exclude the HUD, chat, and video borders. The crop scales for recordings at different resolutions.

Preset excludes the fixed side HUDs (256–1324 at 1920×1080). Check the preview after any recording layout change. Unseen pixels remain transparent; video cannot reveal hidden rooms.

2. Extract & stitch

Start with the Dark SS13 / OS13 preset for the supplied example. Preview a short segment before processing hours of footage.

Advanced motion matching and Z-level detection

Unrecognized views are unlinked map regions, not proof of another floor. A scene return needs agreement across several frames and already-mapped RGB pixels; repeated floor patterns alone do not qualify. The program samples intervening footage to recover missed camera moves and checks other possible overlaps after capture. Match unresolved regions by clicking the same landmark in each.

Use a 10–30 second segment to verify registration. A large mosaic and multiple high-resolution videos require substantial memory.

Distinct map regions remain provisional until matched; they do not necessarily represent separate floors.

The detector cannot know true in-game Z from video alone. An isolated corridor may belong to an existing floor. Avoid assuming all exported region numbers are floors.

To repair a missed connection, click “Connect two matching landmarks,” click a recognizable point on one region, select another region, and click the identical point. Review the proposed match before merging.
