# Deathweb wraith assets

The `ghost`, `pain` and `grue` states from `icons/mob/mob.dmi` and the `ghost1` state from `icons/effects/wraith.dmi` were repacked with their original frames and timing. `sound/os13/wraith/pain_sound.ogg` is `sound/spectre/w_consume.ogg` and `ghost_effect.ogg` is `sound/effects/ghost.ogg`.
