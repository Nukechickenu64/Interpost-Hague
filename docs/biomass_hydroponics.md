Vat hydroponics follows the Hydroponics section of the [Lifeweb Logistics Officer guide](https://lfwb.at/index.php?title=Logistics_Officer&oldid=3443).

On Alpha, three vats occupy the upper hydroponics room and a fourth sits beside the press in the lower room. The north wall has a culture dispenser and Hydroponics Console. The Biomass Press is in the lower room, and three Food Processing Units serve the lower kitchen: pizza on the recipe-book counter, sandwich and shawarma along the south wall. The shawarma unit starts jammed and can be repaired with an empty-hand interaction. A separate real-meat processor sits on the upper kitchen counter. Botanist lockers contain skimmers and starter cultures. Research's head scientist locker contains a Water Chip; Research can also fabricate replacements through the existing engineering research design.

A vat's body occupies both its own tile and the tile directly north of it, and it draws below walking sprites. Workers must stand on the tile directly north of a vat to stir it, add cultures, or skim it; the body tile can only be entered or left through its northern edge, so the east and west sides are walled by the vat itself.

1. Insert the Water Chip into the Hydroponics Console. Click the console with an empty hand to open its live vat-status screen, then select **Fill empty vats** to supply vats in the same room and on the same deck. Alt-click the console to recover its chip.
2. Once a vat is full, add one yeast packet and one glig, in either order. The culture dispenser and cargo supply crates provide replacements.
3. Wait for the flashing red indicator. Click with an empty hand to stir the muck (an empty skimmer also works). Growth stays stopped until someone mixes it; repeat whenever red flashes.
4. When green flashes, use an empty skimmer to scoop one portion of goo.
5. Use the loaded skimmer on the Biomass Press. After it finishes, click with an empty hand to collect the brick. Repeat for the remaining goo.
6. Bring bricks to a Food Processing Unit. Insert a brick, click with an empty hand to activate, then collect the finished meal. The mapped units make ersatz-pizza, sandwiches and shawarma; the cargo-orderable configurable unit also retains Burger, Sausage and Wafers. Alt-click to recover an unprocessed brick. Alternatively, insert bricks into a cloning pod to replenish its existing feedstock supply.

The guide does not give numerical timings or yields. The supplied water-chip video shows five mixes and hand stirring (45:30-46:30). This implementation uses a 10-second fill, five required mixes separated by 30-second growth intervals (including one final interval after the fifth mix), three scoops per batch, and five seconds per pressing or food-processing operation. Each brick supplies 50 cloning biomass; a full batch supplies the existing 150-unit clone cost. A harvested vat needs fresh water, yeast, and glig for its next batch. Power loss pauses machinery; removing the chip pauses filling. A full skimmer cannot mix or harvest again until emptied.

The supplied `vats.dmi` provides vat stages, `os13hydroitems.dmi` provides the skimmer and press, and `hydroponics.dmi` provides the flashing red/green indicators. Existing OS13 cooking/harvest sprites provide yeast, glig, and biomass. Other maps' conventional plant trays keep their existing behavior.

Focused server tests are in `code/unit_tests/biomass_tests.dm`. Compile `biomass_regression.dme` with DreamMaker and run its DMB with DreamDaemon to exercise chip handling, power pauses, recipe consumption, mandatory mixing, bounded harvesting, pressing, food shapes, cloning feedstock, actual machinery processing, and the north-only service position with its blocked upper tile. The wrapper enables machinery during the test setup runlevel; production runlevels are unchanged.

The separate Food Processor accepts real meat, not biomass. Load a slab and activate it to obtain a raw meatball for the existing cooking recipes. Its nutrition and contaminants are preserved. The one-item yield and five-second duration are local balance choices because the video describes this machine without demonstrating a complete meat cycle.

ChargeMasters in cargo convert finite precursor charges into equipment, soda, security, cigarette, bar, chemistry, coffee, hydroponics or snack refills. Click to cycle the selected type, then insert a precursor. Conversion pauses without power. Alt-click to cancel and recover the original precursor. Hydroponics charges restock the culture dispenser. See `os13_waterchip_video_parity.md` for the evidence and regression coverage.
