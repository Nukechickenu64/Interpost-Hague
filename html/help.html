Movement keys require hotkey mode. Use Ctrl with W, A, S, or D to move without hotkey mode.

Interaction

Click objects and people to interact. Use the active hand to equip or use items; drag items between inventory slots as needed. Examine people or objects with Shift and a click.

HUD

The four-part HUD button opens Craft (top-left), Crew (top-right), Help (bottom-right), and Skills (bottom-left).
